Identify & Size Days 1–30
Surface the top 10–15 indirect OPEX spend categories across all Fluent portcos. Assess addressable spend per category using NetSuite + Nexonia data (gap-mapped by Day 30, clean cube by Day 45). Prioritize by spend concentration, vendor fragmentation, and savings potential.
2
Secure MD Buy-In & Commit to a Capture Approach Days 31–60
For each priority category, align with the relevant MD(s) on how the savings will be captured — centralized contract, volume aggregation, vendor consolidation, or rebate model. Every category needs a named MD sponsor who is actively committed, not just aware.
3
Quantify Savings & Deliver the Roadmap Days 61–90
Build a category-by-category savings roadmap: baseline spend, target savings %, annualized dollar impact, and the timeline to realize it on the P&L. At least one category live with spend flowing. This becomes the business case for Year 1 and the template for scaling to new verticals.
